YOUR FIRST PAPER

From your preferences to your paper.

  1. Choose your mix. Open the studio and set your news areas, interests, sources and daily or weekly rhythm. Save on your device.
  2. Research with your assistant. Copy the research prompt into a fresh ChatGPT or Claude chat with browsing. Review the candidate stories.
  3. Bring the paper back. Use the writing prompt in that chat, then import its edition JSON into the studio. Inspect the sources and print or save a PDF.
  4. Review what you read. Rate a specific topic more or less, flag dry writing separately, and undo anything. Suggestions do not change your chosen ratings unless you accept them.

BEFORE YOU START

A few practical things.

Where is my profile saved?

In the browser where you choose to save it. Profiles, papers and reviews are not uploaded to a Press database. Download a backup to move devices. This GitHub Pages guide does not store profiles.

What do I need?

Your own AI account with browsing available, plus a browser. The studio is free. Your provider’s usage limits apply; ink, paper and any publisher subscriptions are yours.

Can it print automatically?

The current beta uses a manual workflow. Scheduling and printer delivery belong to a planned companion, which is not available to buy.

Does feedback lock me into the same topics?

Only explicit topic votes affect learning. Suggestions are the default, adjustments are capped, and reviews can be undone. Reading, skipping and dry-writing feedback do not lower a topic rating.

Can I bring my ChatGPT plan?

The manual workflow already uses your own chat account. A direct ChatGPT plan connection is planned, pending applicable OpenAI approval and local integration testing. See connection status.

How do reviews move between devices?

Take a private phone-review file from Read & print to your phone. Export your reactions and choose Merge reviews only in the desktop studio. This preserves newer desktop interests. A full backup restore is a separate action.

How do I report a problem?

Use the repository’s issues or the Lounge. Share the steps and error message, without personal profiles, newspapers, private feed addresses or account details.
